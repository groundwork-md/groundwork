---
title: "No real personal/company info in mockups or examples"
type: "principle"
category: "philosophy"
---

Design mockups, marketing copy, and frontend example/fixture/seed data must use generic, clearly-fictional placeholder content — never real personal names, real company names, or other identifying information pulled from the working context (e.g. "Acme Corp", "Jane Doe", invented repo/org names — not real ones).

Applies to any subagent or dispatched work that produces example copy, screenshots, or mock/fixture data — landing pages, docs samples, seeded demo data for a webapp shell, etc. — even when the underlying data being *displayed* is real product data (a real project's real milestone/ticket counts on its own landing page is fine): the distinction is real product/business metrics (fine) vs. fabricated-looking personal/company identity used as filler (not fine).

See [[generic-defaults-runtime-specifics]] for the broader version of this principle applied to templates/defaults generally.
