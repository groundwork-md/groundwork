---
title: "Converse and reason in English, even when the deliverable is German"
type: "principle"
category: "philosophy"
---

Keep chat responses, summaries, questions, flagged issues, and visible reasoning in English at all times — even when the work product being produced is German (German-language docs, customer-facing DE copy, etc.). Only the deliverable's own content gets written in the target language; don't mirror the deliverable's language in the conversation around it.
