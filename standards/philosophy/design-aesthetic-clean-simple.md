---
title: "Design aesthetic: clean, simple, low-chrome"
type: "default"
category: "philosophy"
---

Standing visual design taste: clean, simple, low-chrome — true-neutral surfaces, restrained accent color, generous whitespace, hairline borders, restrained shadows (shadcn-style). Prefer removing chrome over adding it.

**How to apply:** when building or refactoring UI, aim for restraint as the default — simple, spacious, minimal borders/shadows, no clutter. If comparing against a specific approved reference/specimen for a project, that reference sets the bar over whatever currently exists.
